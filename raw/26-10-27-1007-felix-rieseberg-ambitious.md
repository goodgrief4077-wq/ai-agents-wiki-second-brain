Blog post dated 13 October 2026 by an engineering manager who works on Claude, arguing that people under-use AI models and should aim higher. It sorts ambition into four types: speed, altitude (ask for the outcome instead of the task), depth (work the way a field-leading expert would, from raw data and with outside validation) and range (bring in methods from several fields). A running example is a company's quarterly business review deck. The closing section links ambition to self-efficacy research and goal-setting studies, with historical examples such as the four-minute mile.
