GitHub front page of the ahtx/kindi repository. The README describes Kindi 0.2.0 as a custom inference engine for Prism ML's Ternary-Bonsai-2 models (2B, 4B, 9B, 27B) that started on a 1 GB Quadro K420 and now covers several older and mid-range NVIDIA cards plus a CPU engine. It offers an OpenAI-compatible server, a one-command installer, and states default output is token-for-token identical to llama.cpp for the same model file. The sidebar shows the AGPL-3.0 licence, about 61 stars and 13 forks, and 6 commits.
